# Indian IPO Market Research: Subscription, Listing Gains & Fintech Brokers

An extension of my MBA thesis on Indian IPOs.

## Data
A dataset of **2,020 Indian IPOs** – Mainboard and SME, NSE and BSE, 2007 to September 2026. Every IPO since January 2024 added from Chittorgarh subscription data, NSE bhavcopy prices and NSE active-client data.

## Analysis
- Subscription by investor category (QIB, NII, retail, employee) vs listing-day gains
- Yearly averages, broker market shares and correlation matrices
- A one-page research brief

## Key findings
- Demand peaked in **2024**: 191x average retail subscription, 53.5% average listing gain
- Listing gains cooled to about **13%** in 2025–26
- Groww's share of NSE active clients rose from **20.8% to 29.2%**, while its correlation with retail subscription fell from 0.95 to 0.62
